## Delete your account

Last updated: 20 October 2026

### Delete your account in the app

Open Peta and go to Settings → Delete account → Delete account. Your data on our server and your Clerk sign-in account are deleted immediately.

### Delete your account without the app

Email support@peta.invalid from the address you used to sign up. Say that you want to delete your Peta account. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. You do not need to reinstall the app.

### What gets deleted

- Your email address, password and sign-in account held for this app by Clerk.
- Everything you entered: your name, Payment Details, Default Currency, Opening Balance, Emergency Fund Target, Subcategories and Budget Items.

### What remains and for how long

- We retain only an internal deleted-account identifier, without your email or any amounts, for up to 3 years, to prevent a request already in flight from recreating the deleted account.
- Crash and error reports already sent to Better Stack are retained for up to 90 days from when they were recorded.

### Questions

Contact support@peta.invalid. For details about how we handle data, see the privacy policy on this website.
