## Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Peta is operated by István Fábián, Carrer del Laberint 2-4, 08005 Barcelona, Spain. Contact: support@peta.invalid or +34 641 582 022. This policy explains how the Peta Android app and this website handle your information.

### Your account

Peta needs an account. You sign up with your email address and a password. Clerk, Inc. handles your email address, password, verification codes and sign-in sessions; we never see or store your password. Our server stores your Clerk user ID.

### What you put into Peta

Our server stores what you enter so that the app can show it to you on any device: your name if you give one, your Payment Details (labels, account holder names, account numbers, IBANs or payment handles), your Default Currency, Opening Balance, Emergency Fund Target, your Subcategories and your Budget Items (planned amounts, currencies, dates and how often they recur). Peta does not connect to your bank and cannot move money.

We process this data to provide the service you signed up for. Our legal basis is performing our agreement with you (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

### Exchange rates

To convert amounts between currencies, our server downloads the European Central Bank's daily reference rates through the Frankfurter API. These requests contain only dates and currencies, never information about you.

### Crash and error reports

The app and server use Better Stack for crash and error reports. Reports can include an error, stack trace, app version, device model, Android version and recent technical events, such as a failed server request. We do not intentionally include your email address, name, Payment Details or amounts. These reports help us diagnose and fix problems. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in keeping the app reliable and secure (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Reports are retained for 90 days.

### Service providers and international transfers

Clerk, Inc. is based in the United States and provides sign-in. Clerk states that it relies on its certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for eligible transfers, with standard contractual clauses as a fallback where applicable. Our NestJS API and PostgreSQL database are hosted by Railway in its EU region in the Netherlands. Better Stack receives error reports, stored in its EU region. These providers process information to deliver their services, not for their own advertising on our behalf. Railway, which also hosts this website, keeps technical request logs that can include IP addresses for a limited time, to run and secure the service.

### Website, advertising and feedback

The website currently uses no visitor analytics or tracking cookies. Its CMS login cookie is for the site's administrator, not visitors. The app currently has no ads, in-app purchases, analytics SDKs or advertising trackers. We do not sell personal data or use it for profiling or automated decisions. We will update this policy before introducing material changes such as advertising or paid features.

If you choose Send feedback, your email app opens with the app version and Android version prefilled. You decide what to send. We use messages you send to respond to you and delete them within 12 months.

### How long we keep data

Your data remains until you delete it or your account. After account deletion, we keep only an internal deleted-account identifier, without your email or any amounts, for up to 3 years, to prevent a request already in flight from recreating the account. Better Stack error reports expire after 90 days. Support emails are deleted within 12 months.

### Children

The app is not intended for children under 14, and we do not knowingly process their personal data. If you believe a child under 14 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

### Your rights and deletion

Under the GDPR, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, portability or restriction of eligible personal data, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Some rights have legal exceptions. Contact support@peta.invalid to make a request.

To delete your account, use Settings → Delete account in the app, or follow the instructions on the Delete your account page of this website. You can complain to the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos at https://www.aepd.es.

### Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date above shows the latest revision. We will explain material changes through an appropriate notice.
